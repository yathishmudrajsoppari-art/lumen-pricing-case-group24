Data & methodology notes

    Personal fields (name, email) from customer_survey.csv are stripped before this app ever sees the data — only aggregated segment statistics are loaded. No API key is required for anything on this page; the optional weather overlay calls Open-Meteo's public endpoint directly from your browser.

    Real-world context we checked, but didn't bake into the model: the case's ~€9.1bn/~7% CAGR German functional-beverage market size lines up with independently published 2026 estimates (Market Research Future, and others converging in the €/$9–9.2bn range) — the market-sizing exhibit isn't an arbitrary number. Separately, German law requires a mandatory €0.25 Pfand deposit on single-use cans like LUMEN's, refunded on return — it's not LUMEN's revenue or cost, but it does raise the amount a shopper hands over at checkout, which is worth flagging for the grab-and-go/kiosk moment the Commuters segment describes, even though none of the survey's price questions appear to have priced it in explicitly.
